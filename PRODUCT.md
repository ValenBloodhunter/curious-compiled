# Curious, Compiled

Personal portfolio of Debashis Das, a B.Tech student at SRM University AP. Intended visitors are collaborators, recruiters, and people interested in his projects.

## Content and actions

- Present the four real projects from `src/data/projects.ts`, with direct GitHub links.
- Explain interests in algorithms, machine learning, software, and hardware.
- Preserve the Codevium 2026 Codecraft Track first-place recognition.
- Provide email, GitHub, and LinkedIn contact routes from `src/data/profile.ts`.

## Current brief

The portfolio follows Ghost Lynx, an original cyberpunk animal, from an interactive close-up to a wide alpine landscape. Flowing motion connects the portrait, vision controls, and project diagrams. Visitors can jump directly to the work, biography, field notes, and contact sections.

## Implementation

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. Responsive single-page navigation. All public contact details and project claims originate in the existing repository. Meaningful text and navigation must work independently of visual motion.
